COVE — CLEARER GESTURES DEMO

Open Cove Gestures.app. This is a separate synthetic playground; the native
SneakPeek app, observer and running jobs are not connected to it.

Press Play demo or Space for the 32-second walkthrough.
Select any scene to stop playback and try it directly.
Replay gesture shows the selected movement again.

WHAT TO LOOK FOR
Needs you: paw lifts high, waves twice, then stays raised. Acknowledge example
clears only this example and returns Cove to watching.
Watching: upright open-eyed bust, quiet after one entrance.
Resting: a horizontal, closed-eye belly-up pose after an explicitly clear queue.
Can't see: a turned back, withdrawal and crossed-eye mark. This never means rest.
Holding paws: TWO otters visibly converge at their central paws, then settle.
Drift: the pair wakes, paws release and one member moves outward and tilts.
Rejoin: the space closes and the paw link is restored.

At a glance previews show the same selected scene at 32 and 64 points.
Otter only shows the selected character OR PAIR as a draggable floating
companion. Click it, Return or Space to restore controls.
To watch all scenes in the floating view, start Play demo and then Otter only.
Command-Q exits only this demo. No idle animation loops. Reduce Motion shows
settled poses immediately.

Gesture recordings contains six GIFs captured from the real SwiftUI renderer.
These GIFs repeat for presentations; the app gestures play once and settle.

BUILD FROM SOURCE
python3 scripts/prepare-cove-source.py
runtime/cove-gestures-source/scripts/test.sh --scratch-path runtime/cove-gestures-tests
scripts/package-cove-gestures.sh

Requires macOS 14+. Packaging requires the Swift toolchain and Pillow for
optional capture inspection; the finished app needs no Python or repository.
The app is locally signed, not notarized. Intel is compiled but runtime/UI
checks were performed on Apple silicon.
